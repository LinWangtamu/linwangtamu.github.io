## 🔬 Selected Research

### Resource-Aware Speculative Decoding for Online LLM Serving
*Jan 2026 – Present*

I investigate how system load and GPU resource constraints affect speculative decoding in online LLM serving. My work focuses on dynamically adapting draft length to reduce end-to-end latency, supported by implementation, benchmarking, and performance profiling in vLLM.

### Stochastic Modeling and Scheduling for Wireless Networks
*Aug 2024 – Present*

I study scheduling over unreliable wireless links, focusing on the trade-off between throughput and Age of Information (AoI). Using stochastic modeling, queueing theory, and Markov decision processes, I develop scheduling policies with theoretical guarantees. This work has led to two first-author papers at ACM MobiHoc 2025 and 2026.

### Real-Time Scheduling for O-RAN
*Aug 2025 – Present*

I develop and evaluate real-time scheduling and control modules on the EdgeRIC platform. This work explores latency and throughput trade-offs under dynamic radio conditions, connecting scheduling algorithms with practical wireless systems.
