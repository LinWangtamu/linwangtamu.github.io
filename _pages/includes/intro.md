Hi! I’m **Lin Wang (王琳)**, a Ph.D. student in Electrical Engineering at **Texas A&M University**, advised by [Prof. I-Hong Hou](https://cesg.tamu.edu/faculty/i-hong-hou/).

My research focuses on **efficient LLM inference** and **wireless network scheduling**. I combine stochastic modeling, optimization, and system implementation to design resource-aware algorithms. My current work explores adaptive speculative decoding for online LLM serving, with an emphasis on how system load and resource constraints affect inference performance.

I received my **B.S.** and **M.S.** degrees in Electrical Engineering from **Nankai University**, China, in 2021 and 2024, respectively.
